About the Project

Hello! My name is Danial Rasouli. I am 13 years old and I am from Iran.

This is my first robotics project. I built and assembled a four-wheel robot using motors, gearboxes, electronic boards, wheels, batteries, and structural parts.

Components

- Main robot board
- Receiver board and four-channel transmitter
- Two motors and two gearboxes
- Four wheels and tires
- Four-battery holder
- Telephone cable
- Structural parts, screws, and nuts
- Electrical wires

Building Process

1. Prepared the main board.
2. Installed the motors and gearboxes.
3. Mounted the receiver board.
4. Repaired a disconnected motor wire.
5. Installed the wheels.
6. Built the robot's structure.
7. Connected the control system.
8. Inserted the batteries and tested the robot.

Problems and Solutions

- Gearbox: Corrected the installation direction.
- Motor wire: Reconnected the wire by soldering.
- Wiring: Corrected a connection and tested the robot again.

Final Result

The robot worked after assembly, wiring, and testing.

What I Learned

I learned about mechanical assembly, wiring, troubleshooting, testing, and problem-solving.
